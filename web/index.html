WHAT THE RESULT MEANS

Your conditions, checked against evidence.

PackSense combines the selected food reference with the conditions you enter, then runs the same validation and package-screening backend used for scenario batches.

From the referenceFood identity, moisture, oil/fat, pH and any reported respiration value. Their source and evidence basis stay visible.
From youTarget life, storage, journey, handling and pack quantity. These are submitted conditions, not independently measured facts.
Possible outcomeAn input exception, a needs-evidence result or a preliminary shortlist. No trained material or shelf-life prediction is deployed.
