A decision in eight steps

01AVAILABLE

Dataset input

Structured scenario rows describe the food, pack quantity, desired life, and storage and transport conditions.

Output Scenario batch
02AVAILABLE

Validate & enrich

Check ranges, units and cross-field consistency. Match food references exactly; keep exceptions separate.

Output Enriched scenarios or exceptions
03EVIDENCE-GATED

Food needs

Build an oxygen, moisture and handling requirement card. Numerical limits require food-specific source approval.

Output Requirement card and gaps
04DIAGNOSTIC ONLY

Fresh-produce path

Consider respiration and local gas and water conditions at storage, transit and excursion temperatures.

Current boundary No MAP safety clearance
05EVIDENCE-GATED

Find packages

Screen complete reviewed structures against food scope, service range, handling and exact transfer evidence.

Output Eligible, excluded or unresolved
